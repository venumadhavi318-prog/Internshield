RISK SIGNAL LIBRARY

What InternShield checks for

The checker is transparent by design. These are the signal categories it looks for in the offer text you submit.

Payment requests

Registration, processing, training, or security fees, and any request for money.

Unrealistic promises

Guaranteed jobs, instant selection, or unrealistic earnings claims.

Informal contact channels

Offers that rely only on WhatsApp or Telegram instead of a verifiable company channel.

Pressure language

Urgency and scarcity wording that discourages independent verification.

Free email addresses

Contact details that use a free mailbox rather than an official company domain.

Sensitive information requests

Requests for OTPs, passwords, bank, or card details — a strong warning signal.

ABOUT INTERNSHIELD

A safety layer for the internship search

InternShield helps students evaluate internship opportunities before they commit — before sharing personal information, documents, or money. It examines an offer for common warning signs and explains what to verify, so you can make an informed decision instead of relying on guesswork.

Clear, explainable results

Every result lists the specific signals that were detected, so you can see exactly why an offer was flagged.

Private by default

Your checks are tied to your own account. Saved history is scoped to you and protected by account-level rules.

Guidance, not a verdict

InternShield provides a risk assessment to inform your decision — it is not a legal or definitive determination.
